## Code of Conduct

This project adopts the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/), version 2.1.

**Our pledge.** We want participation in this project to be a harassment-free, welcoming experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

**Expected behaviour.** Be respectful and constructive; give and accept feedback graciously; focus on what is best for the community and the science; show empathy.

**Unacceptable behaviour.** Harassment, insults, trolling, personal or political attacks, publishing others' private information, and other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

**Enforcement.** Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, issues and other contributions that violate this code, and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors for behaviour they deem inappropriate. Report concerns privately to the maintainer via https://github.com/mdkhademali. Reports will be handled confidentially.

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces and when representing the project in public.
